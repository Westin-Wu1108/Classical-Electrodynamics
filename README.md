# Classical-Electrodynamics
Storage of my own lecture notes on classical electrodynamics, which gives formal proofs and descriptions of the concept in E&M.
These are my personal lecture notes for mathematical physics and classical mechanics.
They are written as part of my own learning process and are shared for students who
are interested in the mathematical structure underlying physical theories.

These notes are not intended to be polished textbooks.
They reflect an evolving understanding and may be updated over time.

If you are a student following my teaching or discussion sessions,
feel free to use them as supplementary material.
